# 15. Portals

Students, faculty and parents each have their own web portal and never use the Desk. All three portals have a new design: a dark red and navy theme, a left sidebar menu, summary cards at the top of each page and a notification bell. Applicant portals (regular, PACE and FLE) are covered in section 4. Login is explained in section 2.

| Portal | Address | Menu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student | `/student-portal` | Dashboard; Academics; Examinations & Results; Fees & Payments; Helpdesk / Services; Announcements |
| Faculty | `/faculty-portal` | Dashboard, My Classes, Attendance, Marks Entry, Venue Booking, Announcements, Helpdesk |
| Parent | `/parent-portal` | My Ward(s), Dashboard, Attendance, Results & Grades, Fees & Payments |

## 15.1 Student portal

| Menu | What students do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | See alerts (courses below 75% attendance, fees pending with **Pay now**), **Active Courses**, **Examination Eligible**, **Fee Outstanding**, next 7 days' classes |
| **Academics** → **Attendance** | Course-wise attendance, minimum, shortfall, exam eligibility; attendance calendar; register for office hours with **Register Attendance** |
| **Academics** → **Courses** | Enrolled courses with credits, next class, venue, faculty |
| **Academics** → **Timetable** | Day, week or month view; **Sync to My Calendar** sends Google Calendar invites |
| **Examinations & Results** → **Exam Schedule** | Exam dates, times and halls |
| **Examinations & Results** → **Results / Grades** | SGPA, term %, course results; **View Marks**; register for an improvement exam |
| **Examinations & Results** → **Grade Appeal** | Appeal a published result |
| **Examinations & Results** → **Re-exam Application** | Register and pay for re-exams |
| **Fees & Payments** | Dues, invoices, receipts; pay online |
| **Helpdesk / Services** | **Helpdesk** tickets, **Fee Certificate**, **Hostel Management** (view only, complaints), **Venue Booking** |
| **Announcements** | Notices tagged New, Urgent or Important |

Staff manage portal announcements and settings from Desk home → **Student Services** → **Student Portal**.

**Pay fees online**

1. Open **Fees & Payments**. The summary shows **Total Levied**, **Settled**, **Scholarship** and **Outstanding**.
2. Click **Pay All**, or **Pay** on one invoice.
3. Check the **Payment Summary** and click **Proceed to Pay**. Pay in the Razorpay window.
4. Download the receipt from the **Receipt** column. To pay cash instead, pay the exact amount at the Bursar's Office with your student ID.

> *Screenshot: Fees & Payments* (see the online manual)

**Download a fee certificate**

1. Open **Helpdesk / Services** → **Fee Certificate**.
2. Select the **Academic Year**, choose a certificate card and click **Download PDF**.

> *Screenshot: Fee Certificate* (see the online manual)

The receipt certificate for scholarships unlocks only when fees are fully paid.

**Register for a re-exam**

1. Open **Re-exam Application**. Eligible courses show the fee and deadline.
2. Click **Pay Online**, or **Pay at Counter (Cash)** then **Confirm Registration** (your seat is held only after paying at the office), or **Register (Free)**.
3. After payment click **Download Receipt**.

> *Screenshot: Re-exam Application* (see the online manual)

**Raise a grade appeal**

1. Open **Grade Appeal**. Under **Raise an Appeal** choose the **Result to appeal** and **Appeal type**.
2. Write your **Reason for appeal** and click **Submit Appeal**. Track it under **My Appeals**; appeals are usually resolved in 5 to 10 working days.

> *Screenshot: Grade Appeal* (see the online manual)

**Book a venue**

1. Open **Venue Booking**. Under **New Venue Booking** fill **Event / Purpose**, **Venue Type**, **Venue / Room**, start and end, and **Expected Attendees**.
2. Click **Submit Booking**. If warned the room is too small or already booked, choose **Change Room** or **Proceed Anyway**.
3. Track it in **My Booking History**.

> *Screenshot: Venue Booking* (see the online manual)

**Submit your bank details (one time only)**

1. Open **My Profile** from the avatar menu and open **Bank Details**.
2. Fill the **Savings Bank Account** section: account number, account holder, bank, branch and **IFSC Code**; optionally attach the passbook.
3. Answer **Availed Education Loan?** and, if Yes, fill the loan details.
4. Click **Submit Bank Details**, check everything and click **Yes, Save**.

> *Screenshot: My Profile with Bank Details* (see the online manual)

You cannot change bank details after submitting. Contact the administration office to reopen them.

**Mark attendance**

1. Open **Attendance** and click **Mark** on one of **Today's Classes** (or **Mark Attendance**).
2. Pick the session in **Select Session**. If it is missing, click **Not seeing your session? Enter details manually**, choose **Based On** and **Date**, and click **Get Students**.
3. Tick present students (**Check all** / **Uncheck all**). The counters show Total, Present and Absent.
4. Click **Save Attendance**. To correct it later, click **View** → **Edit Attendance**.

> *Screenshot: Attendance Management* (see the online manual)

**Recommend a condonation**: in **Condonation Recommendation**, choose **Recommended** or **Not Recommended** for the student's request and click **Submit**.

**Enter marks**

1. Open **Marks Entry** and pick the course under **Select Course**.
2. In **Student Marks**, click the edit icon for a student.
3. Type marks under **Component-wise Marks**. Each mark saves when you leave the field. Values above the maximum or below zero are refused.

> *Screenshot: Marks Entry* (see the online manual)

If you see **Edit deadline has passed** or **Result entry is locked**, ask your Programme Chair or the exam office. **Student identity is masked** means names are hidden for blind marking.

**Profile and password**: **My Profile** → **Edit** to update phone, qualification, specialization, experience and bio, then **Save Changes**. **Settings** → **Change Password**.

## 15.3 Parent portal

1. Log in (section 2). If you have more than one child, click the child's card under **My Wards**; every page then shows that child.
2. **Dashboard**: batch, enrolment number, current term, hostel room, attendance, fees, results and the last RFID swipes (campus in and out).
3. **Attendance**: course-wise attendance marked Good, Low or Critical, and a calendar. Click a day to see its sessions. The bell icon lists **Attendance Alerts** for courses below the minimum.
4. **Results & Grades**: published results with **View Marks**.
5. **Fees & Payments**: dues, invoices and receipts. Click **Pay Now** or **Pay All**, review the **Payment Summary** and click **Proceed to Pay**.
