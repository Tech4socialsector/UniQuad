# 12. Placement

The placement office records companies, publishes opportunities, and tracks applications and offers. Students apply from the portal. Staff need **slcm\_Placement Officer**. Workspace: **Placement** (`/desk/placement`).

**Desk path:** Desk home → **Student Services** → **Placement**.

> *Screenshot: Placement workspace* (see the online manual)

## 12.1 Companies

1. **Company** (`/desk/company/new`): **Company Name**, **Industry / Sector**, **Practice Area**, **Hiring Locations**, **Website**, **Company Status**.
2. **Company Contact**: **Company**, **Contact Name**, **Designation / Role**, **Email**, **Phone Number**, **Primary Contact**.
3. **Company Interaction**: log each call, email or meeting with **Interaction Type**, **Interaction Date** and **Notes**.

> *Screenshot: New Company* (see the online manual)

## 12.2 Publish an opportunity

1. Go to `/desk/placement-opportunity/new`.
2. Choose the **Company**, one or more **Role Name** entries and **Opportunity Type** (Placement or Internship).
3. Fill **Eligibility Criteria**, **Compensation**, **Job Location**, **Application Start**, **Application End** and **Applicable Batches**.
4. Set **Status** to **Open** and click **Save**. Only Open opportunities show on the student portal.
5. Add events (pre-placement talk, test, group discussion) with **Placement Event**: **Placement Opportunity**, **Event Type**, **Event Date & Time**, **Venue**. The date must fall within the application period.

> *Screenshot: New Placement Opportunity* (see the online manual)

> *Screenshot: New Placement Event* (see the online manual)

## 12.3 Applications and offers

1. Students keep a **Student Placement Profile** (CGPA, internships, skills, resume). Staff set **Profile Status** to Verified or Locked.
2. Students apply on the portal; each **Placement Application** starts as **Applied**. A student can apply once per opportunity.
3. Set each application's **Status** to **Shortlisted** or **Rejected**. (**Shortlist Upload** stores the company's shortlist file for reference; statuses are still updated by hand.)
4. Record offers in **Placement Offer** (`/desk/placement-offer/new`): **Student**, **Placement Opportunity**, **Offered Role**, **Compensation**, **PPO**, **Offer Status**. Choosing Accepted or Declined fills **Decision Date**.
