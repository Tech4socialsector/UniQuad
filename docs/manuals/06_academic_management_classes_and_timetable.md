# 6. Academic management: classes and timetable

Once courses are offered (section 3.8) and students enrolled (section 5.6), staff group students into classes and schedule sessions. Use the **Programme Management** workspace (`/desk/programme-management`); timetable work needs **System Manager** or **Academics User**.

**Desk path:** Desk home → **Academics** → **Programme Management**, or Desk home → **Programme Setup**.

## 6.1 Set up a class

1. Go to `/desk/class-configuration/new`.
2. Fill **Class Name**, **Class configuration type** (Section or Group), **Programme**, **Term**, **Batch**, **Year**, **Course**, **Faculty** and **Seat Limit**.
3. Add students with **Add Students by Filter** or **Bulk Upload Students**. The **Students** table also fills automatically from Student Enrollments with the same course, batch and section.
4. Click **Save**.

The **Academic Management** page (`/desk/academic-management`) offers the same through tabs: **Terms** (**Add Term** creates a Term Configuration) and **Class** (**Add Single Class** or **Add Class by Section**).

## 6.2 Build the timetable

1. Go to `/desk/timetable-configuration`. A weekly calendar opens; filter by **Term**, **Course** and **Type**.
2. To add a single session, use the **Time Table** form at `/desk/time-table/new`: choose **Based On**, the **Class Configuration**, **Faculty**, **Course**, **Schedule Date**, **From Time**, **To Time** and **Venue**.
3. To repeat it, set **Repeat** (Daily, Weekly or Monthly) and **Repeats Till**.
4. Click **Save**. The session shows on the calendar and in the student and faculty portals.

## 6.3 Upload a whole timetable

1. On the Timetable Configuration page click **Upload Timetable** and pick a **Programme**.
2. Click **Download Class List** to get a CSV template of the classes.
3. Fill in the sessions, attach the file in **Upload CSV** and click **Upload** (System Manager only).
4. Click **Download Report** any time to export the timetable.
