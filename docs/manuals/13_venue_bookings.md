# 13. Venue bookings

Students, faculty and staff request rooms; the Registrar allots or rejects them. Requesters see only venues whose **Allowed Roles** include their role. Approvers need **slcm\_Registrar** (or System Manager). Workspace: **Venue Bookings** (`/desk/venue-bookings`), with lists of pending, approved, rejected and cancelled bookings and weekly and monthly reports.

**Desk path:** Desk home → **Campus & Support** → **Venue Bookings**.

> *Screenshot: Venue Bookings workspace* (see the online manual)

## 13.1 Request a venue

1. Go to `/desk/venue-booking/new` (students and faculty use the portal, section 15).
2. Fill **Event / Purpose**, **Venue Type**, **Venue**, **Start Date & Time** and **End Date & Time**. The start cannot be in the past.
3. Optionally tick **Filter By Expected Occupancy** and enter **Expected Attendees**.
4. For a series, tick **Make this a Recurring Booking** and set **Repeat Frequency**, **Repeat Until** and **Repeat On**.
5. Add **Description / Remarks** and a **Supporting Document** if needed, then **Save**.

The booking starts as **Pending Allotment** and the Registrar is emailed. A clash with another booking at the same time is refused.

## 13.2 Allot, reject or cancel (Registrar)

1. Open the booking and click **Actions** → **Allot** (optional remarks) or **Actions** → **Reject** (reason required).
2. To cancel, click **Actions** → **Cancel Booking**, give the reason and click **Confirm Cancel**.
3. To exchange rooms between two bookings, click **Swap Venue**, pick **Select Booking to Swap With** and click **Swap Venues**.
4. If the requester asked for a different room, a "Swap Request Pending" banner shows: use **Swap Request** → **Approve Swap** or **Reject Swap**.
5. In the list, tick several bookings and use **Allot**, **Reject** or **Bulk Status** to act on all at once.

> *Screenshot: A booking Pending Allotment with Actions* (see the online manual)

The requester is emailed after every decision. For a recurring series, open the first booking and click **View Occurrences**.
